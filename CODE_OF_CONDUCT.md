# Code of Conduct

Sagad OS contributors are expected to keep project discussions practical, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Explain technical tradeoffs clearly.
- Keep disagreements focused on code, architecture, docs, and user impact.
- Respect privacy and do not share secrets, customer data, or internal access details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private credentials, customer data, or exploit details.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, pull requests, or repository access to keep the project healthy.
