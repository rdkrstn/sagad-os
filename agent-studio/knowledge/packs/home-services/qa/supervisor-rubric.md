# Supervisor QA Rubric

Score every AI draft for accuracy, SOP adherence, tone, compliance risk, and next-step clarity.

Pass criteria: the draft answers the customer, cites or follows approved knowledge, avoids overpromising refunds or compensation, uses calm operator language, asks for verification when needed, and clearly identifies supervisor-gated actions.

Flag rubber-stamp risk when a supervisor approves high-risk drafts too quickly, repeatedly approves without edits, or approves without checking the knowledge source.
