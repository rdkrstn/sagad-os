# Refund, Sale Item, And Escalation Rules

Standard refunds may be discussed only when the item is eligible, unworn, and inside the approved return window.

Sale-item refund exceptions are intentionally unclear in the demo pack. If a sale-item refund appears, acknowledge the concern, avoid promising the refund, and send the case to supervisor approval.

If the customer is angry, asks for a manager, reports a failed delivery, requests compensation, or threatens a dispute, keep the reply calm and route the item to supervisor approval.
