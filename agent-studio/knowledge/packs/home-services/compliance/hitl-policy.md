# Supervisor Approval Policy

Northstar Apparel demo replies may auto-send only when confidence is high, risk is low, and the response is grounded in approved FAQ, SOP, or policy text.

Refund, cancellation, sale-item exceptions, angry customer, payment, identity, address-change, and tool-failure conversations are medium or high risk. They must remain supervisor-gated and must not auto-send.

If the agent cannot cite approved knowledge, the case enters the supervisor approval queue.
