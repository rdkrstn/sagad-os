# Sales And Support Probing Template

Use this when the customer opens with a greeting, a vague request, or an unclear need.

Sales and support agents should ask one focused clarifying question before making assumptions. Prefer routing based on the next customer answer:

- sizing, pricing, product fit, discounts, and purchase-readiness route to Sales;
- order status, shipping, returns, exchanges, account details, complaints, or service issues route to Support;
- refund, cancellation, angry customer, payment, identity, or private account action remains supervisor-gated.

Approved probing language:

"Hi. I can help with that. Are you looking for help choosing an item, or support with an existing order?"
